About Lukadde FC

Our History

Founded with a passion for the beautiful game, Lukadde FC has grown from a local community team to a competitive force on the pitch. Our journey has been built on the sweat and determination of our players and the unwavering support of our fans.

Who Are We?

Lukadde FC is a football club from Lukadde, Kampala.

We train young players and play big matches.

Our colours are Blue and Light Blue.

Our Philosophy

Our motto, "We struggle together," is more than just a slogan. It is the core of our club's DNA. We believe that through teamwork, resilience, and mutual support, we can overcome any challenge and achieve greatness together.



The Crest

Our logo represents the heart of the club. The shield signifies our defense and unity. The red silhouette of the striker represents our aggressive, forward-thinking style of play, while the soccer ball and streaks of gold capture the energy and glory we strive for in every match.
